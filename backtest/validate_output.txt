=== HONEST CAVEATS ===
This finding has the following potential confounders:
 1. SAMPLE: 339 trades is meaningful but not huge. Bootstrap CI tells you the range.
 2. SLIPPAGE: We're assuming you can fill at the close-of-candle yes_ask. In reality,
    when YES drops to 40c with tight spread, the resting offer may evaporate.
 3. SURVIVORSHIP: We only see markets that traded — markets with no morning volume
    at 40-49c never appear, but might be where the real mispricing lives.
 4. NO MODEL: This says the price band has +EV — but in real-time the bot still
    needs a rule for WHICH 40-49c market to enter. Indiscriminate entry on every
    40-49c market means LOTS of trades — sizing matters.
 5. WINDOW: Mar-May 2026 only. Different seasons have different dynamics —
    summer extremes, winter swings, transition periods all behave differently.
